# AegisVoice AI — The Simple Guide (Explained for 6th Graders!) 🦸‍♂️🛡️

Imagine a high-tech superhero shield that protects your grandparents from robot tricksters on the phone. Here is how it works, step by step!

---

## 🎭 The Big Problem: The "Robot Voice Mask"

Imagine you post a 5-second video on TikTok or YouTube. 
A bad guy can download your video, feed your voice into a computer, and make a **robot copy of your voice** (called an **AI Voice Clone**). 

Then, the bad guy calls your grandmother at 11 PM:
> *"Grandma! It's me! I fell off my bike and I'm at the police station. Please send \$1,000 right now or they won't let me go home! Don't tell Mom!"*

Grandma hears YOUR exact voice crying. She gets super scared, runs to the bank, and sends the money. By the time she realizes you were actually safe in school, the money is gone forever.

**AegisVoice AI** is the superhero shield that stops this from happening!

---

## 🕹️ Step 1: Log In (Pick Your Superhero Character)

1. Open the website: [**`https://build-to-ship-seven.vercel.app/login`**](https://build-to-ship-seven.vercel.app/login)
2. Look at the cards under **"Instant Demo Logins"**:
   - 👵 **Eleanor Vance**: The 78-year-old sweet grandmother we want to protect!
   - 👨‍💼 **Marcus Vance**: Her son who wants to make sure his mom is safe.
   - 🛡️ **Dana Sterling**: The bank security officer.
3. Click **Eleanor Vance** and click **Sign In**. Boom! You are inside the secret command center.

---

## 👂 Step 2: The "Can You Spot the Robot?" Game

1. On the main page, find the game with **Sample A** and **Sample B**.
2. Press **Play** on both:
   - One is a real kid asking about grandma's peach cobbler.
   - The other is a robot voice pretending to be in trouble.
3. Guess which one is the fake robot! 
4. Most humans get tricked, but AegisVoice spots the robot in **less than 1 second** by noticing tiny robotic glitches human ears can't hear!

---

## 🔐 Step 3: The Secret Family Vault (Voice Enclave)

Think of this like building your family's secret treehouse club with a secret handshake:
1. Click **Voice Enclave** at the top.
2. Click **Enroll Voice**:
   - Your real granddaughter talks into the microphone for 10 seconds.
   - The computer learns her real voice: how high or low she talks, and how fast her vocal cords vibrate.
   - **Super Privacy Rule**: AegisVoice **never** saves your voice recording. It only saves math numbers and throws away the audio!
3. Click **Add Secret**:
   - Write a secret question only your real family knows:  
     *"What was the name of our fluffy dog at the lake?"*
   - A robot scammer calling from another country will never know that secret!

---

## 🚨 Step 4: The Danger Screen (The Call Guardian HUD)

When a phone call comes in, click **Call Guardian HUD**:
1. **The Green Wobbly Line (Oscilloscope)**: Shows the sound waves of the voice. If a robot is talking, the waves look jittery and robotic!
2. **The Danger Speedometer (Risk Gauge)**:
   - 🟢 **Green (0 - 29)**: Safe! It's really your family.
   - 🟡 **Yellow (30 - 59)**: Hmm, sounds a little suspicious.
   - 🔴 **Red (85 - 100)**: **DANGER!** A robot clone is trying to steal money!
3. **The Bad Word Catcher**:
   - As the caller talks, words like `"jail"`, `"bail"`, `"urgent"`, and `"send money"` light up in bright flashing red!

---

## 🦸‍♀️ Step 5: The Two Superhero Moves

When the meter hits the **Red Zone**, grandma doesn't have to panic. She has two superhero buttons:

### 1. The Trick Question Button ("Ask Challenge")
Grandma clicks **Ask Challenge**. The screen tells her to say:
> *"Before I send any money, what was the name of our dog at the lake?"*

The robot clone stutters and says: *"Uhh... I don't remember, grandma, just send the money!"*  
Busted! Caught red-handed!

### 2. The Big Red "Hang Up" Button (Kill Switch)
Grandma clicks the big red **Terminate Call** button. 
- The call gets cut off immediately!
- The computer automatically sends a text message to her son Marcus:  
  *"Emergency: We just stopped a fake scam call targeting your mom. She is safe!"*

---

## 🕵️‍♂️ Step 6: The Detective Report (Forensic Audit)

After the call hangs up, click **Compile Audit Report**:
- It shows a cool timeline you can slide back and forth like a YouTube video.
- It proves to the police and the bank that the caller was a fake robot and shows how much money was saved!
